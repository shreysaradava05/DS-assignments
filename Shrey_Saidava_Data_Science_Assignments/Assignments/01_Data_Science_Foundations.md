## 1. App and a data-driven feature

## 2. Data science problems solved by apps
| App | Data science problem | How it helps |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | Content recommendation | Shows posts and reels based on interests. |
| Google Maps | Route and traffic prediction | Suggests routes with less traffic. |
| PhonePe | Fraud detection | Finds unusual transactions and may flag them. |
| Spotify | Music recommendation | Suggests songs based on listening history. |
| Zomato | Restaurant recommendation | Suggests restaurants based on ratings and orders. |

## 3. Three uses of data science
1. **Healthcare:** helps study patient data and identify patterns.
2. **Banking:** helps detect unusual or fraudulent transactions.
3. **Transport:** helps estimate traffic and travel time.
