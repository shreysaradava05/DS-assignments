## 1. Test a new checkout page
- **Null hypothesis (H₀):** the new checkout page does not change the average number of completed orders per day.
- **Alternative hypothesis (H₁):** the new checkout page changes the average number of completed orders per day.

I would compare data from similar time periods, while accounting for factors such as promotions and traffic. I would choose a significance level of 5% before analysing the results.

## 2. Decision at a 5% significance level
If the p-value is less than 0.05, I reject the null hypothesis. If the p-value is 0.05 or higher, I do not reject the null hypothesis. Not rejecting H₀ does not prove that it is true.
